Outreach in the Truckee community

We have a goal to provide guidance and STEM opportunities to the local community. One of the major parts of our team is to engage with those interested in STEM and our engineering process, and we love to give back to the community.

We're open to discussing STEM opportunities for our team as well as providing them to the community. If your school, group or event would like us to visit and share what we do, or if you know of a program, mentor or opportunity that could help our students learn and grow, we'd love to hear from you.

Truckee Maker Show

We bring our robots to the Truckee Maker Show at the Truckee Community Center, where people of all ages can see them up close, ask questions and learn what robotics involves.

Robot building

Students design, build and program our competition robot together at team build sessions. Newcomers work alongside experienced members and learn CAD, Java programming and electrical systems hands-on. No experience is required.
